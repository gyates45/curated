# notes/ — evergreen notes

The actual asset. Everything else in the system exists to feed this folder.

Rules (enforced by taste, checked by the weekly review):

- **Atomic**: one idea per note. Three ideas = three notes, linked.
- **Claim-shaped titles**: `curation-is-the-work.md`, not `notes-on-curation.md`.
  If the filename can't be argued with, it isn't a claim yet.
- **Your own words.** Highlights quote other people; evergreen notes are what
  *you* now think. Short attributed quotes only.
- **Sourced**: frontmatter `sources:` traces where it came from, or marks it
  original thinking.
- **Linked**: every note appears in at least one MOC (`mocs/`), or it's an
  orphan the monthly review will catch.
- **Status is maturity, not quality**: `seed` (AI-drafted or quick) →
  `growing` (revised once) → `evergreen` (you'd publish from it). Only Greg
  promotes.
